Pricing

Prepaid, pay as you go, no subscription. You pay a few micro-dollars when something costs us money — issuing, renewing, online decisions — and nothing for offline verification.

Prepaid credit

Add at least $5.00 by card. The card processor's fee is itemized on top, so you receive the full credit. Unused credit never expires while your account exists.

Integer micro-dollars

Balances and prices are whole micro-dollars (µ$): $1 = 1,000,000 µ$. Every billable call reserves its price first and never charges more; a zero balance gets a 402, never a debt.

Free where it should be

Offline verification, revocation (prepaid at issue), JWKS, status lists, the SDK, the developer tools and dashboard reads are free.

Prices

Live from the service. Each price must pass the price-floor rule: price × 0.92 ≥ 1.5 × worst-case cost (0.92 is what is kept after refunds and disputes; 1.5 is the minimum margin), checked by a test over every price.

Card fee example

The fee is ceil((credit¢ + 30) / 0.971) − credit¢: a $5.00 credit is charged $5.46. New accounts may add at most $20 per payment during their first week.

Cost calculator

Estimate a month from the same live prices. Devices using the SDK or CLI renew automatically at two thirds of their credential's lifetime.

Who pays

  • The issuing account pays to issue and renew its devices' credentials.
  • The site (audience) that asks pays for online decisions and Sign in with devicebasedauth.org.
  • The caller pays for reads made with an API key or a device that is not signed in to the dashboard.

Offline verification never reaches us, so it is free no matter how much you use it. See the docs for how each operation is metered.
