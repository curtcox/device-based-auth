The service

devicebasedauth.org issues signed, revocable credentials that bind a device's public key to a list of permissions (grants) for one or more websites or APIs, publishes revocation status, and answers optional online authorization and sign-in requests. Devices generate and keep their own private keys; we never receive them. You decide which devices to approve and what they may do. Sites that accept credentials decide for themselves whether to rely on them.

Location and time constraints are evidence, not proof: network location can be changed with a VPN or proxy, and device-reported location is only as honest as the device. Do not rely on a credential's location constraints where being wrong would cause serious harm.
