What we never have

Device private keys. Devices generate their keys and send us only the public half; we cannot sign as your devices.

What we keep

  • Account: your sign-in identity from our sign-in provider (Clerk) and your account id.
  • Devices and credentials: public keys, device names and kinds you or your devices chose, the credentials we issued (including their grants), and their issue, renewal and revocation history. An expired credential is deleted 13 months after it was issued, or 30 days after it expired if that is later; a device goes once it has no credential left and nothing was issued to it for 13 months (a revoked device is kept, so its key stays refused); account history and charges older than 13 months are deleted too, except deposits and monthly totals.
  • Online decisions: when a site asks us to decide a request, the time, site, action, resource, result and the country the request came from, kept for 90 days and visible to the device's owner. Finer location (region, coordinates, IP address) supplied by the site or the device is used for the decision and not stored.
  • Sign-ins: short-lived authorization codes, deleted within minutes of use.
  • Billing: your balance and ledger. Card details are handled by our payment processor (Stripe); we receive payment status and amounts, not card numbers.
  • Operations: rate-limit counters and aggregate cost measurements. [LEGAL REVIEW: request logs, retention and subprocessors (Cloudflare, Clerk, Stripe).]

What credentials reveal

A credential is readable by anyone it is shown to. Every site a device presents it to can see the device name, your account id, the sites it is valid for, and its grants — including any places and time windows. Choose generic device names and coarse places when that matters. Status lists reveal only which numbered entries are revoked.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data, show advertising, or build profiles of where people go. We do not allow the service to be used for covert tracking of people (see the Terms).

Your choices

You can read every credential and decision in your dashboard, revoke anything at any time, and ask us to export or delete your account. [LEGAL REVIEW: data-subject rights, response times, lawful bases, international transfers, children's data, and the dormant-account closure policy.]
